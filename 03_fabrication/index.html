Fabrication

For week 3, we were assigned to prototype a kinetic sculpture powered by a single motor. We were introducced to Arunio IDE programing in class where we learned the basics of curcuitry and soldering. I experienced lead soldering through attaching pins to the microcontroller.

Kinetic Sculpture

My idea for the kinetic sculpture is a ship that moves up and down on one plane to represent it riding on ocean waves. I used the skill I learned from creating my box to create the base platform for my model.
